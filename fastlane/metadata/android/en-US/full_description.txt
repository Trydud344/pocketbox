Pocketbox hosts a private OpenCode server on your Android phone, inside an Alpine Linux userland (via PRoot, no root required). Connect to it from the same device with your favorite OpenCode remote-access client at http://127.0.0.1:4096.

FEATURES
• One-tap setup: downloads and checksum-verifies the pinned Alpine rootfs and OpenCode binary
• Always-on server via a foreground service with supervision and automatic restart
• Loopback-only binding with a mandatory randomly generated password
• Pairing screen: copy URL/username/password (sensitive clipboard), QR for other devices
• Built-in tools: git, ripgrep, curl, ssh, optional node/python packs
• Logs, diagnostics (pb-doctor), bounded one-shot command runner
• Quick Settings tile, start on boot, battery-optimization exemption

PRIVACY
• No telemetry, no analytics, no ads — ever
• Downloads only from pinned HTTPS URLs with pinned SHA-256 checksums
• Everything runs in the app's private sandbox on your device
